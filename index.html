The spaces

Not just one hall

There's more than one room under this roof. The main hall carries the bones of the old church nave: high ceilings, an open floor, and a stage end that's already rigged for performance thanks to its theatre conversion. Beyond it, the building holds further rooms of different sizes, plus a kitchen, so you can book the whole premises for something big, or just the right-sized room for something small.

Exact layouts, capacities and rates for each space are available on request. Every hire starts as a conversation, not a price list. Tell us what you're planning and we'll tell you what fits.

Our story

From congregation
to community hub

Wanstead Methodist Church closed its doors as a worshipping congregation a few years ago. The building is now looked after by the Forest Methodist Circuit, which cares for ten Methodist churches across Waltham Forest, Wanstead and Loughton.

Rather than let the building sit empty, the Circuit hires it out. It's part of a wider belief that opening up church premises helps build stronger, more connected neighbourhoods. We'd love your group to be part of what happens here next.
